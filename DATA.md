# Data sources, tiers and attribution

Every record carries a tier:
- **OBSERVED:** measured or reported.
- **MODELLED:** reanalysis or satellite products.
- **SIMULATED:** generated by us.

The UI, the video and the API output show the tier.

| Layer | Tier | Source | Licence | Where in the repo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stream geometry, culverts, direction | OBSERVED | OpenStreetMap via Overpass | ODbL 1.0, © OpenStreetMap contributors | `data/reaches/*.geojson` (edges, nodes) |
| Places near streams (parks, playgrounds, schools) | OBSERVED | OpenStreetMap | ODbL 1.0 | `data/reaches/*.geojson` (role=place) |
| Hourly rain and temperature | MODELLED (ERA5-based) | Open-Meteo | CC BY 4.0 | `data/cache/openmeteo_*.json` |
| Candidate outfalls | **SIMULATED** | Placed by `place_synthetic_candidates` (OSM maps almost no outfalls) | ours | `synthetic: true` on every candidate |
| Citizen reports and check results in the C-014 replay | **SIMULATED** | `dipper_engine.scenario` | ours | `tier: "simulated"` in the ledger |
| SourceBench incidents | **SIMULATED** | `dipper_engine.sim` | ours | `data/sourcebench/results.json` (label SIMULATION) |

## Personal data

- Observers are pseudonymous ids. Never store names, emails or phone numbers.
- Photos are re-encoded without EXIF (including GPS) and detected faces are blurred before they are stored or sent to the vision model. If face detection is unavailable, the photo is not sent. Number plates are not yet redacted. Stored photos are in `data/media/` (gitignored).
- Suspected outfalls are never linked to addresses in public outputs.
